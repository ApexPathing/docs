# Manual Tuning
---
Apex takes pride in our ability to tune your robot's constants automatically, however, the process isn't fool-proof
(despite our best efforts to make it otherwise). **The first thing to do if something goes awry is to submit a bug report/issue
on our [Discord](https://discord.gg/5qGVTdYrDV). If you don't have Discord, a [Google form](https://forms.gle/BiTs1Q3yozKYmu966)
is also available.** Notifying us about the issue can directly help other teams have a better experience as we work to
refine this tool to be among the best pathing libraries for FTC.
